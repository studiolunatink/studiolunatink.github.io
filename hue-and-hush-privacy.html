Hue & Hush Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Hue & Hush mobile game (the "Game") developed by LunaTink. In short: the Game does not collect any information about you.

Data we collect

None. The Game does not collect, store, share or sell any personal or non-personal data, such as your name, email, location, device identifiers, advertising ID or usage statistics.

Internet connection

The Game never connects to the internet. It runs entirely on your device and does not send data to any server.

Ads and third parties

The current version of the Game contains no ads and does not include any third-party services such as analytics, advertising, tracking or crash reporting. If ads are added in the future, this policy will be updated beforehand and will clearly state what data is processed, by whom and why.

Permissions

The Game does not request any special permissions on your device (camera, microphone, location, contacts, files and so on).

Information stored on your device

Your progress (such as finished paintings and settings) is stored only on your own device, inside the Game's own storage. It never reaches us. Uninstalling the Game removes it.

Children's privacy

Because the Game collects no data at all, it collects no data from children. Players of all ages can play it with peace of mind.

Content

All paintings, the icon and the logo in the Game were drawn from scratch. The music is generated live on your device while you play.

Changes

If we change this policy, the updated version will be posted on this page and the date above will be updated.

Contact

If you have any questions about this policy, write to us: studio.lunatink@gmail.com
